## 👥 Creating Your First Group

### What is a Group?

A group is a collection of people who share expenses together. Examples:
- **Roommates** - Rent, utilities, groceries
- **Trip** - Vacation expenses
- **Event** - Party, dinner, concert
- **Project** - Shared purchases

### How to Create a Group

1. **Click "Create Group"** on dashboard
2. **Enter group details**:
   - **Name**: e.g., "Paris Trip 2025"
   - **Description** (optional): "Spring break adventure"
   - **Add members**: Enter email addresses or select from contacts
3. **Set roles** (optional):
   - **Owner**: Can delete group (you by default)
   - **Admin**: Can add/remove members, edit group
   - **Member**: Can add expenses, view data
4. **Click "Create"**

### Inviting Members

**Option 1: By Email**
- Enter email addresses when creating group
- Members receive invitation email
- They sign up and auto-join group

**Option 2: Share Link**
- Click "Invite" in group settings
- Share invitation link via text, social media
- Anyone with link can join

**Option 3: In-Person**
- Have them open the app
- You scan their QR code (Settings → QR Code)

1. **Click "+" button** or "Add Expense"

1. While creating expense, click "Upload Receipt"
2. Take photo or select from library
3. We'll automatically extract:
   - Amount
   - Date
   - Merchant name
4. Review and confirm details

## 🔀 Split Methods Explained

### Equal Split (Default)

**When to use**: Everyone shares equally

**Example**:
- Hotel: $300
- 3 people
- Each pays: $100

**How it works**:
```
Total ÷ Number of people = Amount per person
$300 ÷ 3 = $100 each
```

---

### Exact Amounts

**When to use**: Different people owe different amounts

**Example**:
- Dinner: $100
- Alice ate $40 of food
- Bob ate $30 of food
- Carol ate $30 of food

**How to set up**:
1. Select "Exact Amounts"
2. Enter exact amount for each person
3. We verify total equals expense amount

---

### Percentage Split

**When to use**: Split based on percentage

**Example**:
- Rent: $2000
- Alice has large room: 50%
- Bob has medium room: 30%
- Carol has small room: 20%

**How to set up**:
1. Select "Percentage"
2. Enter percentage for each person
3. We verify total equals 100%
4. We calculate amounts automatically

---

### By Shares

**When to use**: Proportional split

**Example**:
- Grocery run: $150
- Alice gets 3 shares (family of 3)
- Bob gets 2 shares (couple)
- Carol gets 1 share (single)
- Total shares: 6
- Cost per share: $150 ÷ 6 = $25
- Alice pays $75, Bob pays $50, Carol pays $25

**How to set up**:
1. Select "Shares"
2. Enter number of shares for each person
3. We calculate cost per share
4. We calculate each person's amount

## ✅ Settling Up

### What is Settling Up?

When someone pays you back, you "settle up" to record the payment.

### How to Settle Up

**Option 1: Smart Settlements** (Recommended)
1. Go to group → **"Settle Up"** tab
2. View **settlement suggestions**
3. We show optimal payments to minimize transactions
4. Click **"Mark as Paid"** when payment received

**Example**:
```
Before optimization:
- Alice owes Bob $50
- Bob owes Carol $30
- Carol owes Alice $20

After optimization (1 payment instead of 3):
- Alice pays Carol $30
```

**Option 2: Manual Settlement**
1. Go to group → **"Settle Up"**
2. Click **"Record Payment"**
3. Enter:
   - **Who paid**: Payer
   - **Who received**: Payee
   - **Amount**: Payment amount
   - **Date**: Payment date
   - **Method**: Cash, Venmo, PayPal, etc.
   - **Note** (optional): Confirmation number
4. Click **"Save"**

### Payment Methods

Record how you paid:
- Cash
- Venmo
- PayPal
- Zelle
- Bank Transfer
- Check
- Other

This helps both parties remember payment details.

## 👥 Managing Groups

### Group Settings

Access via group → ⚙️ Settings:

- **Edit group name/description**
- **Add/remove members**
- **Change member roles**
- **Group photo**
- **Notification settings**
- **Export data**
- **Leave group**
- **Delete group** (owner only)

### Adding Members

1. Group settings → **"Members"**
2. Click **"Add Member"**
3. Enter email or share link
4. Set role (Admin or Member)

### Removing Members

1. Group settings → **"Members"**
2. Click member → **"Remove"**
3. Confirm removal

⚠️ **Note**: Can't remove members with outstanding balances. Settle up first.

### Changing Roles

1. Group settings → **"Members"**
2. Click member
3. Select new role:
   - **Owner**: Full control (only one owner)
   - **Admin**: Manage members and expenses
   - **Member**: Add expenses, view data

### Leaving a Group

1. Group settings → **"Leave Group"**
2. Confirm you want to leave

⚠️ **Note**: You must settle all balances before leaving.

### Deleting a Group

**Owner only**:
1. Group settings → **"Delete Group"**
2. Confirm deletion

⚠️ **Warning**: This permanently deletes all expenses and history. Consider exporting data first.

## 💡 Tips & Best Practices

### For Roommates

1. **Create one group** for all shared expenses
2. **Add recurring expenses**:
   - Rent (monthly)
   - Utilities (monthly)
   - Internet (monthly)
3. **Settle up monthly** or quarterly
4. **Use equal split** for most things
5. **Use percentage split** for rent if rooms are different sizes

### For Travel

1. **Create group before trip**
2. **Add all travelers** before departure
3. **Track as you go** - Don't wait until end
4. **Upload receipts** for reimbursement
5. **Settle up at end** of trip or monthly
6. **Use equal split** for shared activities
7. **Use exact amounts** for personal purchases

### For Events

1. **Create group early** when planning starts
2. **Track all planning costs**:
   - Venue deposit
   - Supplies
   - Food/drinks
   - Entertainment
3. **Add attendees** as they confirm
4. **Equal split** for per-person costs
5. **Settle up** after event

### General Tips

✅ **Do**:
- Add expenses immediately
- Upload receipts for large expenses
- Settle up regularly (monthly/quarterly)
- Communicate with group members
- Use clear descriptions ("Dinner at Mario's" vs "Food")

❌ **Don't**:
- Wait until end of month to add expenses (you'll forget)
- Add expenses to wrong group
- Forget to include everyone who should split
- Leave groups with unsettled balances

## ❓ FAQ

### General

**Q: Is SplitTab free?**
A: Yes! The MVP features are free during beta. Premium features (receipts OCR, advanced analytics) may be added later.

**Q: What platforms do you support?**
A: iOS app and web app (desktop + mobile). Android coming soon.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. We use bank-level encryption, secure servers, and never store payment information.

---

### Groups

**Q: How many groups can I create?**
A: Unlimited during beta. No restrictions.

**Q: Can I be in multiple groups?**
A: Yes! Be in as many groups as you need.

**Q: What happens if I leave a group?**
A: You lose access to group data. Your expense history is preserved, but you can't view or edit.

**Q: Can I recover a deleted group?**
A: No. Group deletion is permanent. Export data before deleting.

---

### Expenses

**Q: Can I edit an expense?**
A: Yes, if you created it. Click expense → Edit. Changes notify group members.

**Q: Can I delete an expense?**
A: Yes, if you created it. Click expense → Delete. This recalculates all balances.

**Q: What if I entered the wrong amount?**
A: Edit the expense and update the amount. Balances will recalculate automatically.

**Q: Can I add an expense from the past?**
A: Yes! When creating expense, set the date to any past date.

---

### Settlements

**Q: Do you integrate with Venmo/PayPal?**
A: Not yet. You handle payment outside the app, then mark as paid in SplitTab. Payment integration coming in Phase 2.

**Q: What if someone pays partial amount?**
A: Record multiple settlements. Example: Owe $100, paid $50 → Record $50 settlement, balance now $50.

**Q: Can I undo a settlement?**
A: Yes, within 24 hours. Click settlement → "Undo". After 24 hours, record a new expense to reverse.

---

### Balances

**Q: Why don't balances match my calculations?**
A: Check:
- All expenses included?
- Split method correct?
- Any settlements recorded?
Still wrong? Contact support with details.

**Q: How does settlement optimization work?**
A: We use algorithms to minimize number of payments. Example: Instead of 6 payments between 4 people, we suggest 3 optimized payments.

**Q: Can I see balance history?**
A: Yes. Group → Balances → Click any balance → "History" shows how balance changed over time.

---

### Account

**Q: How do I change my email?**
A: Settings → Profile → Email. Verify new email before change applies.

**Q: I forgot my password**
A: Login screen → "Forgot Password" → Enter email → Check email for reset link.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Settings → Privacy → "Delete Account". Settle all balances first. Deletion is permanent after 30 days.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes. Settings → Privacy → "Export Data". Receive email with CSV files of all your expenses.

---

### Technical

**Q: The app is slow/crashing**
A: Try:
1. Update to latest version
2. Restart app
3. Clear cache (Settings → Advanced → Clear Cache)
4. Contact support if persists

**Q: I'm not receiving notifications**
A: Check:
1. Settings → Notifications → Enabled?
2. Device settings → SplitTab → Notifications allowed?
3. Email notifications → Check spam folder

**Q: My receipt upload failed**
A: Ensure:
- Image size < 10MB
- Format: JPG, PNG, PDF
- Internet connection stable
Try again or contact support.
