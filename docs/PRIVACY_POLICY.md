## 6. How We Protect Your Data

### 6.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures:

**Technical Safeguards**:
- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Encryption at rest (AES-256)
- Password hashing (bcrypt)
- Secure authentication (JWT tokens)
- Regular security updates

**Administrative Safeguards**:
- Access controls (least privilege)
- Employee training
- Background checks for employees with data access
- Incident response procedures

**Physical Safeguards**:
- Secure data centers (SOC 2 compliant)
- 24/7 monitoring
- Redundant systems

---

### 6.2 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

**Immediate deletion**:
- Access to your account
- Session tokens

**30-day retention** (recoverable if you change your mind):
- Account data
- Expenses and groups you created
- Personal information

**90-day retention** (for legal/security purposes):
- Logs and security data
- Backup copies

**Indefinite retention** (anonymized):
- Aggregated analytics
- Non-identifiable usage statistics

---

### 6.3 Data Backups

We maintain regular backups for disaster recovery:
- Daily automated backups
- Stored securely with encryption
- Retained for 30 days
- Then permanently deleted

---

### 6.4 Third-Party Security

Our service providers maintain their own security certifications:
- SOC 2 Type II compliance
- ISO 27001 certification
- GDPR compliance (where applicable)

### 6.5 Security Limitations
